## Copy package

- `copy/shopee-listing-vi.md`: complete Shopee title, highlights, description, FAQ, and chat templates.
- `copy/facebook-posts-vi.md`: eight publish-ready Facebook posts.
- `copy/tiktok-scripts-vi.md`: three short-form video scripts with captions and shot lists.
- `copy/content-calendar-14-days-vi.md`: a practical two-week publishing plan.
- `copy/video-storyboard-vi.md`: the Google Flow video concept and overlay timing.
